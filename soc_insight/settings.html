Acknowledgement SLA thresholds (minutes)

Configurable demo thresholds, not universal SOC standards. Acknowledgement time = acknowledged_at − created_at; for unacknowledged incidents, now − created_at. Strictly greater than the threshold is a breach; ≥80% of it is "At risk". No resolution SLA is defined.

About SOC Insight

Supervisory analytics for SOC incident records: severity, SLA compliance, analyst workload, trends and explainable recommendations.

Stack: Python 3, Flask, SQLite, Bootstrap 5, Chart.js, Font Awesome.

Demo-data notice: all data is synthetic (documentation IP ranges 203.0.113.0/24 and 198.51.100.0/24). The app is not connected to a real SOC, SIEM or threat feed and does not detect attacks; it analyses incident records supplied to it.

Timestamps are stored and shown in UTC. Local demo app without login: do not expose it to a network.
